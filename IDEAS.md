# Ideas and inspirations

Everything the owner has said they like, from other games and elsewhere, gathered from the chats so nothing gets lost. `BACKLOG.md` holds the work plan; this file answers two questions: what did we take from each game, and what's still to add.

Status: ✅ built · 🟡 partly built · ⬜ not yet · ❌ what we avoid from that game

## By game

### Star Wars Jedi: Survivor (the template for saber combat)
- ✅ Third-person saber combat: five-hit combo, a heavy combo, four dual-saber combos, block and parry
- ✅ Force powers: push, pull, choke, lightning, saber throw
- 🟡 Combos: plenty is the goal ("simple but plentiful"). We have a handful; free animation sources are the limit (Mixamo is broken)
- ⬜ More stances, including a proper reverse grip (Ahsoka)
- ✅ **Saber duels:** duellists block from the front, bat away shots, and stagger when parried or when their guard breaks (v29)
- ✅ **The dismemberment mod:** the blade kills on touch, cuts where it passes, hands and arms come off and the soldier keeps fighting, a cut leg makes him crawl
- ⬜ Cuts that follow the blade's angle (a slanted slice, not at a joint)
- ⬜ Droids that actually come apart when you slice them (sparks, not blood); droids are set aside for now

### Lego Star Wars (including The Skywalker Saga)
- ✅ The little blade trail as the saber moves
- ✅ Force targeting: a red marker on whoever you look at, with a switch to hide it in crowds
- ✅ Fun over everything; cartoon blood
- ⬜ **Character switching** (swap between your characters)
- ❌ Enemy health bars (The Skywalker Saga added them): never

### Call of Duty
- ❌ **Zombies' bullet sponges** (round 20, 1,000 bullets per zombie). Every enemy dies fast; difficulty comes from numbers
- ⬜ A **zombies mode** built that way: more and more zombies, never tougher ones; zombies keep crawling after losing their legs
- ✅ Snappy aiming (with Fortnite)
- ❌ Health bars on enemies (Black Ops Cold War): never

### Modern Warfare 2 (Spec Ops)
- 🟡 **Enemy types, not skins:** infantry with pistols, AKs, M4s and snipers are in
- ✅ **Juggernauts:** a skin-tight force field, three saber hits to drop it, walks at you firing; a sticky kills him outright (v29)
- ⬜ **Dogs**
- ⬜ **Killstreaks,** e.g. a support gunship
- ⬜ **"Big Brother":** co-op where one player runs through enemies while the other covers them from above

### Black Ops 2
- ✅ Quickscoping: the sniper scope snaps in fast
- ⬜ Trickshot mechanics, as an option

### Halo
- ✅ **Jackal-style energy shields:** the shield covers his front; get round him or break it (v29)
- ✅ Juggernaut force fields (see MW2)

### Minecraft
- ✅ The only limit is inventory slots: no weight, ever
- ✅ A hotbar you scroll through (six slots, centred)

### Fortnite
- ✅ Scroll wheel and number keys to switch

- ✅ Teleport orb (thrown, you appear where it lands)
- ✅ Grappling hook (the plunger idea), on soldiers too

- ✅ Attachments from the item's own menu (suppressor, scope)

### Red Dead Redemption 2 and GTA V

## Other ideas (not tied to a game)
**Saber**
- ✅ It lights up faces and the ground; it scorches and sparks when it cuts the world; hum, swing and ignite sounds
- ✅ Saber and blaster as one item (the blaster overheats after six shots)
- ⬜ **Choose how you carry and draw it:** hip, cross-draw, back
- ⬜ **Customisable sabers,** saved as items you find again next time
- ⬜ Clash effects (saber on saber)

**Movement**
- ✅ Triple jump (each jump higher), wall run during the super sprint, slides, double jump, jetpack, ground pound
- ✅ **Fall damage by class:** the Force class shrugs it off, deadly ledges are striped, soldiers die from high falls (v29)
- ⬜ Mechs (someday)

**Combat and enemies**
- ✅ Soldiers drop their guns and you can pick them up; each body has about three mags
- ✅ Difficulty modes (easy to extreme; smarter AI on the hard ones); slow health regeneration
- ✅ Sticky grenade (kills outright), fire grenade (blocks a path with fire)
- ✅ Wave missions with escalating weapons: pistols, AKs, ARs, snipers
- 🟡 Enemy chatter and screams; your own recorded voice lines *(v37: your recording is in: spotting, reloading, pain, man down, grenades, fire, barrels, taunts. Calm patrol chatter still to record)*

**Story and world**
- ⬜ A light story ("go take out these people"), a villain, silly cutscenes you can walk around in (even out of frame a little)
- ⬜ **The city:** ancient-Roman / Naboo style, with close-quarters and sniper areas
- ⬜ **Duo co-op** (PvE only)

**Animation and presets**
- ⬜ **First person** with aim down sights and leaning around corners
- ✅ **Move presets:** favourites (★), "Always my favourites", three packs (v29)
- ⬜ A better pistol model
