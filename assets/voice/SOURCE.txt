Soldier voices: recorded by the owner (all rights theirs), for this game.

raw/v1_owner_session1.m4a  voice v1, session 1 (6 min 40 s, phone recording, 2026-10-02): every soldier's voice for now.
raw/v1_owner_session2.m4a  car, 2026-10-02: deaths, cut-off screams, searching, reloading, muttering, radio, disguise lines.
raw/v1_owner_session3_songs.m4a  car: humming and singing to himself. These are snippets of real songs: fine for a
                           personal game, but replace them before any sale.
raw/v1_owner_session4.m4a  car: more reloading and deaths.
v1_takes*.txt              where each take is in those recordings, by category (cut lists for tools/make_voice.py; each
                           names its recording on an @source line).

Build: python3 tools/make_voice.py v1 assets/voice/v1_takes*.txt
       -> build/vo/v1/<category>_<n>.ogg, embedded by tools/build2.py as __ASSETS.vo.v1.
A new voice: record it the same way (say "this is me saying <category>" quietly, then the takes loud, with a pause
between), add raw/v2_….m4a and v2_takes.txt, run make_voice.py v2 …; soldiers then pick v1 or v2 each.
