build/civilians.glb (v35): the civilians, a man (civ_m) and a woman (civ_f).

- Bodies, eyes, eyebrows and hairstyles (SimpleParted, Buzzed, Beard, Long, Buns, BuzzedFemale; the "Rigged to Head
  Bone" glTF versions): Quaternius "Universal Base Characters", CC0 1.0.
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
- Clothes: the Male_Peasant and Female_Peasant outfits from Quaternius "Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy", CC0 1.0.
  https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html (itch: quaternius.itch.io/modular-character-outfits-fantasy)
- Made with: node tools/make_civilians.mjs <Universal Base Characters zip> <Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy zip>.
  It keeps only the uncovered parts of each body (head and neck, and the woman's hands), binds clothes and hair to the
  body's skin, simplifies the dense meshes, shrinks the colour maps to WebP and compresses with meshopt.
